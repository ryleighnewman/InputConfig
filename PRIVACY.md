# Privacy Policy

InputConfig never transmits anything. Everything it records stays on your Mac.

Settings, presets, calibration data and usage statistics are stored locally, inside the app's own container. The usage statistics are counts the app keeps for its own Statistics panel: how long a controller was connected each day, which presets were activated and for how long, and how many times each button was pressed. They never leave your Mac, and Reset in the Statistics panel clears them.

To do its job the app reads the controls you have mapped: controller buttons and sticks, MIDI messages, and, for rows bound to them, keys and clicks from the Mac's own keyboard, mouse, and trackpad. Input is matched against your presets as it happens and is not recorded, apart from the per-control counts above and a short activity log of the actions it fired, which stays in memory and is saved only if you save it yourself.

For automatic preset switching the app notes which app is in front, to compare it with the apps you listed. That name is not stored or sent anywhere.

Tips in the tip jar are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. The app learns only whether a purchase went through, never your payment details.

If you choose to save or share the activity log or a crash report, it contains the app's own actions (which button fired which output, with any text it types, website it opens, or Shortcut it runs counted in characters rather than written out), your preset and device names, your settings, and when presets started, stopped, or switched. It goes only where you send it.

The app makes no network calls in normal use. There are no analytics, no crash reporting services, no tracking, and no accounts. The only links that leave the app are the ones you click yourself, such as the source code on GitHub or the tip jar on the Mac App Store, which Apple handles.

The full policy is at https://inputconfig.com/privacy
